# Picking Price User Guide

This guide is for buyers and for anyone new to Picking Price. Sellers should also read the [Seller Guide](SELLER_GUIDE.md).

Picking Price connects buyers and sellers directly. You can browse products that sellers have listed, or post a request for what you need and let sellers send you offers. The site does not take payments or arrange delivery: you agree the deal with the other person, inspect the item, and pay them yourself.

## 1. Create an account

1. Open the site and choose **Signup**.
2. Enter your name, your mobile number, your email address, a password of at least 8 characters, and the same password again in **Confirm Password**. The mobile number is required and is saved in your profile, where you can change it later.
3. Choose how you want to use the site: buying only, selling only, or buying and selling. You can change this later in your profile.
4. Tick **I accept the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service** (both are links you can open and read first). The account cannot be created until the box is ticked.
5. Press **Create Free Account**. When the site sends email, a **Confirm Your Email** step follows: a 6-digit code is emailed to the address you entered. Type it in and press **Confirm Email**; the account is then ready and you are signed in. The code is valid for 15 minutes; if it does not arrive, check the spam folder or press **Resend code** (offered again after 45 seconds). **Use a different email** takes you back to the form.
6. If you leave before entering the code, log in later with your email and password: a new code is emailed and the same step opens. Resetting your password with **Forgot Password** also confirms the address.
7. You can also sign up with your Google account by pressing the **Google** button, when it is offered. The box above is needed only when you sign up with the form, and a Google account does not need a code because Google has already confirmed its email address.

Accounts created before email confirmation was introduced keep working as before; they are not asked for a code.

You can browse products and requests without an account. You need to sign in to save products, chat, post a request, send an offer, write a review or report something. When you try one of these while signed out, the site takes you to the login page and brings you back afterwards.

**Forgot your password?** Open **Forgot Password** on the login page:

1. Enter the email address of your account and press **Send Code**. A 6-digit code is emailed to you; check the spam folder if it does not arrive.
2. Enter the code and press **Verify Code**. The code is valid for 15 minutes and works once.
3. Choose a new password, type it again and press **Update Password**. Then log in with the new password.

If you registered with a phone number, or the email does not arrive, contact support (see **Contact Us** in the footer). They give you a code that you enter through **Already have a code from support?** on the same page.

## 3. Search and filter

1. Type what you want in the search box and press **Search**. You can pick a category first.
2. The results appear at the top of the home page (the slider, categories and every other section stay below them), with these tools above them:
   - **Sort by**: Relevance, Newest first, Price low to high, Price high to low.
   - **Type**: new and pre-loved together, new only, or pre-loved only.
   - **Condition**: New, Like New, Good, Fair or Used.
   - **Location**: type a city or area; it matches inside the seller's location, so "Peshawar" also finds "University Town, Peshawar".
   - **Price**: a minimum and a maximum in rupees.
3. Use **Reset filters** or **Clear search** to start again.

The **New Products**, **Pre-Loved** and **All Categories** pages have a filter panel for category, area, condition, price, brand, and delivery or pickup options, with their own sort menu.

## 4. Look at a product

Open a product to see its photos, price, condition, brand, quantity available, location, when it was listed, the description, delivery and pickup options, and the seller's name, rating and number of active listings. Product cards show each photo whole, never cropped. On the product page the photos form a slider: swipe on a phone, drag with the mouse, or use the arrows, the dots or the small photos underneath.

From the product page you can:

- **Save it** with the heart icon. It appears in your Wishlist.
- **Share it** with the share icon.
- **Chat** with the seller inside the site.
- **Make an Offer**, when the seller is open to offers: enter the price you want to pay and it is sent to the seller in the chat.
- **Propose Exchange**, when the seller is open to an exchange: describe what you would give and it is sent to the seller in the chat.
- **Contact** the seller by phone, when the seller has added a number.
- Open **View Seller Profile** to see the seller's other products and reviews.
- **Report this listing** if something is wrong with it.

## 5. Post a request

Use a request when you cannot find what you need. Sellers see it and send you offers.

1. Choose **Post a Request**.
2. **Step 1**: write a title, set the quantity, choose a category and sub-category, and choose the condition you accept (Brand New, Like New, Good, Fair or Used).
3. **Step 2**: enter your budget, add notes and reference photos if you have them, enter the **city or area** and choose how you prefer to receive the item.
4. Submit. Your request now appears on the **Requests** page.

Every request is **live for 30 days**. Request cards show how long is left (for example *30 days left*, or *Ends in 5 hours* on the last day), on the Requests pages and in your Buyer Dashboard. If no offer has been selected when the 30 days are over, the request is taken down automatically and you get a notification (and an email) saying so: *No offers came in* when nobody made an offer, or *No offer was selected* when offers came but you did not choose one. Editing a request does not change when it ends.

**Save Draft for Later** keeps an unfinished request. You will find it under **Draft Requests** in your Buyer Dashboard, where you can continue or delete it.

If your account is for selling only, the site asks you to switch on buying first.

## 6. Receive and choose offers

1. When a seller sends an offer you get a notification, and the request shows **Responses Received**.
2. Open the request from your dashboard to see every offer with its price and details. You can sort the offers and chat with each seller.
3. Choose **Select seller** on the offer you prefer. That seller is told they were selected and the others are told they were not.
4. You can also **Close request** when you no longer need it, or **Cancel request**. A request is taken down by itself after 30 days if no offer was selected.

In your Buyer Dashboard, under **My Requests**, every request shows the date it was **posted** and the date it **expires**, and has its own buttons:

| Button | When | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Edit Request** | While it is live | Change the details; the end date stays the same |
| **Close** | While it is live, or after you selected a seller | Marks it as done; no more offers |
| **Cancel** | While it is live | Stops it; no more offers |
| **Post again** | After it ended, was closed or cancelled | Posts it again as a new request, live for another 30 days |
| **Delete** | Always | Removes it for good; sellers with an open offer on it are told |

## 7. Messages

Every chat is about one product, request or offer, and the chat shows that item at the top with a **View** button. Open **Messages** to see all your chats and reply. The number beside **Messages** is how many messages you have not read yet, and each chat with new messages shows its own number; both go away once you open the chat. A chat opened from a notification appears in a popup that fits the screen, with the message box always in view.

Under each message you send there is a tick: one tick means it was sent, two ticks mean the other person has opened the chat and seen it. An open chat updates by itself every few seconds.

If someone sends you something abusive or suspicious, press the small flag beside their message to report it.

## 8. Reviews

After you have chatted with a seller you can rate them from 1 to 5 stars and write a short review, either at the bottom of their product page or on their profile. You can have one review per seller; saving again updates it. Your reviews are listed under **My Reviews** in the Buyer Dashboard.

## 9. Your Buyer Dashboard

The dashboard shows your profile, your requests and their status, your draft requests, the reviews you wrote, your saved products and your recent chats. Use the pencil on your photo to edit your profile: name, photo, location, phone, a short description, and whether you use the site for buying, selling or both.

## 10. Notifications

You are notified when a seller sends an offer, when you receive a message, when your request is taken down after 30 days, when a report you sent has been reviewed, and when the support team changes something on your account or content. If the site has email switched on, the same notifications are also sent to your email address.

After each change you make (saving to your wishlist, sending an offer, posting a request and so on) a short message at the bottom of the screen says what was done. Opening a chat or a notification does not show one.

**Emails you receive** (when the site has email switched on): the code that confirms your email address when you sign up, a welcome email once the account is confirmed, the code when you use Forgot Password, a notice when your password has been changed (if you did not change it, reset it straight away), and a copy of your notifications. For chats you get at most one email per conversation every 15 minutes, so a busy chat does not fill your inbox.

Opening the Notifications page marks them all as seen, so the number on the bell goes away; the ones that were new stay highlighted until you leave the page. Opening a chat also clears that chat's message notifications.

## 11. Report a problem

- **A product, request, message, review or member**: use the **Report** link shown on it and explain what happened.
- **The site itself**: use **Report a Problem** in the footer.

The support team reviews every report and you are notified of the result.

## 12. Stay safe

- Meet in a busy public place in daylight.
- Inspect the item fully before you pay.
- Do not send money in advance to someone you have not met.
- Keep the conversation in the site's chat so there is a record.
- Report anything that looks like a scam.

More advice is under **Safety Tips** and **Help Center** in the footer.

## 13. About the technology

Picking Price is a web application. The pages are built with React 19.2.6 (Vite 8.0.13), the server runs on Node.js 24 with Express 5.2.1, and all data is kept in a PostgreSQL 18 database on Railway. "Continue with Google" uses Google OAuth 2.0, and emails are sent through the Gmail API. The full list of components and versions is in the project's README.
