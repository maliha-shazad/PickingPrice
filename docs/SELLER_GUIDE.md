# Picking Price Seller Guide

This guide is for members who sell on Picking Price. For creating an account, searching, chat and safety, see the [User Guide](USER_GUIDE.md).

Selling on Picking Price is free. The site takes no commission: the buyer contacts you, inspects the item and pays you directly.

## 1. Switch on selling

Choose **Selling** or **Both** when you sign up. If you registered for buying only, the site asks you to switch on selling the first time you press **Sell Now**; choose **Buying and selling** and continue. You can change this at any time under **Edit Profile**.

When your account can do both, use the **Buying / Selling** switch in the header to move between the Buyer Dashboard and the Seller Dashboard.

## 2. List a product

The description (**About this item** on the product page) can be up to 1000 characters; a counter under the box shows how many you have used.

1. Press **Sell Now**.
2. Add up to six photos. The first photo is the one shown in lists.
3. Enter the title, category and sub-category, and the brand if there is one.
4. Choose the condition honestly:

   | Condition | Meaning | Shown in |
   |---|---|---|
   | New | Unused, in its original packing | New Products |
   | Like New | Used once or twice, no visible wear | Pre-Loved |
   | Good | Minor marks, fully working | Pre-Loved |
   | Fair | Visible wear, works as it should | Pre-Loved |
   | Used | Heavy wear, may need a small repair | Pre-Loved |

5. Enter the price in rupees and the quantity you have. Fields marked with a red star are required; the page tells you which one is missing when you publish.
6. Write a description: age, what is included, any faults.
7. Enter your location, and optionally a phone number, a WhatsApp number and a preferred meet-up spot.
8. Tick the delivery and pickup options you offer.
9. Decide the two deal switches (see below).
10. Check the **Live Preview** of your product card, and choose **Standard (Free) Listing** or **Premium Listing** (see section 5). On a computer both are on the right of the form; on a phone or tablet they come under the form, just above the Publish button.
11. Publish. A standard listing is live immediately.

### Deal switches

| Switch | When it is on |
|---|---|
| **Allow Offers** | The product page shows "Open to offers" and "Negotiable: Yes", and buyers get a **Make an Offer** button. Their offer arrives in the product's chat as a message with the amount |
| **Open to Barter / Gadget Exchange** | The product page shows "Open to exchange" and buyers get a **Propose Exchange** button. Their proposal arrives in the product's chat |

With both switches off the product is shown as fixed price. You can change the switches at any time by editing the listing. Offers and proposals are only messages: nothing is agreed until you reply and the two of you settle it.

## 3. Listing slots

Every seller has a number of free listing slots (10 unless the administrator has changed it). Each active listing uses one slot; removing a listing frees its slot.

When your slots are full, the site tells you and you can ask for more:

1. Open the Seller Dashboard → **Quota & Services**.
2. Choose a pack (+5 or +10) or enter the number of extra listings you want. The amount to pay is shown (Rs. 50 per slot unless the administrator has changed it).
3. Pay that amount using the payment instructions shown in the form, then fill in the payment details our team checks against the transfer: how you paid (JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer or other), the transaction ID, the sender's name and account or wallet number, the amount you paid, the date, and a screenshot or photo of the receipt. The amount must not be less than the price, and each transaction ID can be used once.
4. Submit the request.
5. The administrator checks the payment. When it is approved the slots are added and you are notified; if it is rejected you are told why.

While a slot payment is waiting for verification and your slots are full, publishing a new listing tells you so; you can publish as soon as the payment is approved.

## 4. Manage your listings

Open the Seller Dashboard → **Active Listings**. For each product you can view it, edit it, promote it or remove it. Use the Active and Closed tabs to see what is live and what is not.

If the support team hides one of your listings you receive a notification with the reason.

Buyers can review you after they have chatted with you. Reviews appear on your products and your profile as cards with the stars, the date and the text, under your average rating. Each product counts its **views**: one per visitor per day, and your own visits are not counted. The most viewed product on the site is shown on the home page as the **Popular Product**.

## 5. Promote a product (Premium Listing)

A promoted product is a **Premium Listing**: it carries a **Premium** badge and is shown before ordinary products for the time you pay for.

1. In **Active Listings**, press **Promote** on the product.
2. Choose one of the three lengths on offer (by default 24 hours, 3 days or 7 days) and see the price.
3. Pay that amount using the payment instructions shown in the form, then fill in the payment details our team checks against the transfer: how you paid (JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer or other), the transaction ID, the sender's name and account or wallet number, the amount you paid, the date, and a screenshot or photo of the receipt. The amount must not be less than the price, and each transaction ID can be used once. Then submit.
4. When the administrator approves the payment the promotion starts. It ends by itself, and you are notified when it does.

You can also ask for a promotion while you create the listing: on the Sell Now page choose **Premium Listing**, pick the length, pay, fill in the payment details (with the receipt screenshot) and press **Publish as Premium Listing**. A free listing (the other option) goes live at once. The listing is saved but **not shown to buyers yet**: your Seller Dashboard shows it as *Waiting for payment verification*. When the administrator approves the payment it goes live with the Premium badge. If the payment is rejected you are told why; send the payment again with **Promote**, or press **Publish as free listing** to make it live as a normal listing.

Promoted products also appear in the **Premium Products** section of the home page, above Live Buyer Requests.

## 6. Answer buyer requests

Buyers post what they are looking for on the **Requests** page.

1. Open a request to read what the buyer needs: title, category, budget, quantity, condition, location and how many days are left (a request is live for 30 days). If the buyer deletes a request you made an offer on, you are notified and the offer is closed.
2. Press **Submit Offer**, enter your price and describe what you are offering. You can add a photo.
3. Use **Chat with Buyer** to answer questions.
4. Follow your offers under **My Offers** in the Seller Dashboard. You can edit or withdraw an offer while the request is still open.
5. You are notified when the buyer selects your offer, or when another seller was selected.

A buying-only account cannot send offers; switch on selling first.

## 7. Messages with buyers

Every chat is tied to the product, request or offer it is about, and that item is shown at the top of the chat. Reply from **Messages**. Report an abusive message with the flag beside it.

## 8. Your profile and reviews

Buyers see your profile from your products: your name, photo, location, description, verified badge, number of active listings, your rating and your reviews. Keep it complete under **Edit Profile**.

A buyer can review you only after the two of you have been in contact. Your average rating appears on your products and at the top of your Seller Dashboard. If a review breaks the rules, use **Report review** on it.

The **Verified** badge is given by the administrator.

## 9. Seller Dashboard at a glance

| Tab | What is there |
|---|---|
| Active Listings | Your products, with edit, promote and remove |
| Quota & Services | Slots used and free, extra slot requests, promotions and payment history |
| My Offers | Offers you sent on buyer requests |
| Received Offers | Offers sent to your own requests |
| My Requests | Requests you posted as a buyer |
| Edit Profile | Your public details and account type |

## 10. Notifications sellers receive

- A buyer sent you a message.
- Your offer was selected, or another seller was selected.
- Your listing limit was reached.
- A slot or promotion payment was approved or rejected.
- A promotion ended.
- A buyer reviewed you.
- The support team warned you, or hid or restored one of your items.

If the site has email switched on, these are also sent to your email address.

## 11. Good practice

- Use clear, real photos of the actual item.
- Describe faults honestly; it avoids disputes when you meet.
- Reply quickly in chat.
- Meet in a busy public place and count the payment before handing over the item.
- Remove a listing once the item is sold so buyers are not disappointed and your slot is freed.

## 12. About the technology

Picking Price is a web application. The pages are built with React 19.2.6 (Vite 8.0.13), the server runs on Node.js 24 with Express 5.2.1, and all data is kept in a PostgreSQL 18 database on Railway. "Continue with Google" uses Google OAuth 2.0, and emails are sent through the Gmail API. The full list of components and versions is in the project's README.
